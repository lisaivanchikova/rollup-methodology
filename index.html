Methodology — AI Value Migration v3.0

How Bobroff Cap selects roll-up verticals in the AI tsunami · 2026-05-23
In one paragraph. We don't pick "good industries" — we pick cells (sub-sector × workflow) where (1) AI dislodged measurable value from an incumbent, (2) we can pocket it instead of an upstream gatekeeper, (3) the market adopts AI within our investment horizon, and (4) we can actually buy a way in. The product of those four conditions = cell score. Vertical-level verdict = aggregate of cell scores: a sub-sector is "Hell-Yes" if ≥2 cells score in Hell-Yes range. Sum of all green cells = the fund's total addressable opportunity.

The formula

Scell = V × C × A × B Cell score = Value Created × Capture Share × Adoption Speed × Buyability

Two layers of the map

Layer 1 — Sub-sector cards answer the executive question: "Should we invest in this vertical?" Each card aggregates all cells in that sub-sector. Vertical verdict rules: Hell-Yes if ≥2 cells score ≥3000; Strong if Σ S ≥ 8000 or max cell ≥ 3000; Conditional if Σ S ≥ 3000; Pass / KILL below.

Layer 2 — Workflow matrix answers the operational question: "After we acquire an operator, where do we deploy the AI playbook?" A vertical can be "Hell-Yes" overall but have one specific workflow that's red (e.g. PropMgmt landlord relationship is always red — but that's by design; the playbook deploys on rent collection, leasing, scheduling). The matrix shows where AI plays well vs. where it doesn't.

Why multiplication, not summation

Summation would let a strong V compensate for a weak C. That's the mortgage processors trap: V is huge (banks automate claims, save hundreds of millions per year), but C ≈ 0 — five banks own the borrower relationship, every saved dollar flows upstream to them. Sum would falsely greenlight this. Multiplication punishes any weak component: 9 × 9 × 9 × 0.5 = 364 (Pass), not 27 (which a sum would give).

The four multipliers

VValue Created — what AI dislodged

What
$/year that AI displaces from incumbent in this cell nationally. Normalized to 0–10 scale.
Formula
V = normalize(LR + ΔM)
LR
Labor Removed = labor cost × workflow share × avg task replaceability. Karpathy-grade because κ values are measured, not guessed.
ΔM
Margin/Revenue uplift = revenue × (margin uplift + speed uplift × contribution margin). From P&L Before/After AI sheet (16 verticals).
Sources
BLS QCEW · Celonis/UiPath catalogs · Karpathy.ai/jobs + Anthropic Economic Index · P&L sheet
Why V exists: if LR + ΔM ≤ 0, there's nothing to extract. BPO × Customer Support has V=10 (AI removes 90% of agents) — but C zeroes it because value flows to direct-to-enterprise AI vendors, not BPO operators.

CCapture Share — what we pocket

What
% of V we keep. 0–10 scale.
Formula
C = 10 × π_dist × π_moat
π_dist
Distribution Control ∈ [0,1]. 0 = upstream gatekeeper owns customer (banks in mortgage; OEMs in parts; aggregators in handyman). 1 = operator owns customer directly.
π_moat
Moat strength ∈ [0,1]. License + proprietary data + physical presence + multi-sided network.
Why C is multiplicative inside itself: distribution and moat are both necessary. Strong distribution + no moat → generic AI bypasses us. Strong moat + weak distribution → value flows to whoever owns customer.

AAdoption Speed — how fast market accepts

What
Speed of AI adoption in workflow, in years until 50% market adoption. Faster = more $/year extracted before commoditization.
Formula
A = 10 / t_50
Calibration
t_50 = 1 yr → A = 10 (claims doc parsing). t_50 = 2 yr → A = 5. t_50 = 5 yr → A = 2 (HomeCare scheduling). t_50 = 10 yr → A = 1 (surgery).
Why A: Nicolas's "$/time". Total fund $ = ($/year) × (years in window). Slow adoption + fast commoditization = window opens too late.

BBuyability — can we enter

What
Whether Bobroff can actually acquire operators. Replaces "fragmentation as gate".
Formula
B = 10 × (TD × OW × PD) / (1 + CFD)
TD
Target Density: log(N operators in our $1–10M EBITDA band).
OW
Owner Willingness: silver tsunami × succession gap × distress.
PD
Price Discipline: (Public comp − Asking) / Public comp.
CFD
Competition For Deals: log(1 + active PE platforms).
The Agrosignal test: Russian agri-software had one company on market. TD low. But OW=1, PD=1, CFD=0 → B high. Old S16 "fragmentation ≥ thousands" would have blocked. New formula passes correctly.

Worked example

Insurance Brokerage P&C tier-2 × Document parsing

V = 9LR ≈ $3.6B/yr nationally (doc parsing κ=0.95, fraud κ=0.80). ΔM ≈ $1.1B/yr. Total ≈ $4.7B/yr.
C = 6.4π_dist = 0.85 (broker owns client). π_moat = 0.75 (50-state license + accumulated book).
A = 8t_50 ≈ 1.25 yrs: high pressure (savings obvious), moderate friction (compliance).
B = 8.5tier-2: 145K agencies, low PE saturation, strong silver tsunami, median asking 5.5×.
S = 9 × 6.4 × 8 × 8.5 = 3,917 — Hell-Yes 💚
Vs Insurance tier-1: Same V=9, same A=8, but C drops to ~3.5 (PE-saturated), B to 3 (CFD high). S = 9 × 3.5 × 8 × 3 = 756. Five times lower — same NAICS, opposite verdict. Sub-segment definability handled mathematically.

What's NOT in the formula — and why

Fragmentation as hard-gate
Absorbed into B via TD. Agrosignal one-target case passes correctly.
CAGR / TAM standalone
CAGR is a metric of static economies. TAM is implicit in LR and ΔM.
"Profitable at SME level" gate
Absorbed into V. If LR + ΔM > 0, the cell creates extractable value.
"Why this time is different"
If V > 0, AI is rewriting the cell's rules — that is "why now".
Founder access
Not in this formula. About the deal, not the vertical class. Verified when shortlisting top-10 cells.
Geography (US vs EU)
Workflow is the same. PropMgmt UK vs US is deal-level. One map, deal localizes.
Anti-pattern as separate gate
Catchment-bound, asset-heavy, COGS-dominant all mathematically fail on V or B. No separate gate needed.

Honest weaknesses

1. Normalize(V) is subjective. Anchor points may shift as we add cells.

2. t_50 is estimated. S-curve velocity has ±50% error band.

3. π_moat is a composite. Four moat types compressed into one number.

4. Map is static, economy isn't. Recompute quarterly.

Working cycle

  1. Quarterly — refresh grid: L_S (BLS), κ (Anthropic Economic Index), PitchBook PE-saturation.
  2. Per cell — collect 5 atomic inputs. 30–60 min per cell.
  3. Compute S — formula applies.
  4. Aggregate to vertical-level verdict — sum + max + Hell-Yes count.
  5. Rank verticals by Σ S — focus on Hell-Yes + Strong.
  6. Top-10 only — full workflow audit: P&L bridge, named operators, founder access.
