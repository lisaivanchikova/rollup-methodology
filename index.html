Methodology — AI Value Migration v3.0

How Bobroff Cap selects roll-up verticals in the AI tsunami · 2026-05-23
In one paragraph. We don't pick "good industries" — we pick cells (sub-sector × workflow) where (1) AI dislodged measurable value from an incumbent, (2) we can pocket it instead of an upstream gatekeeper, (3) the market adopts AI within our investment horizon, and (4) we can actually buy a way in. The product of those four conditions = cell score. Sum of green-cell scores = the fund's total addressable opportunity. No 27 criteria, no "intuitively good" verticals — just measurable math.

The formula

Scell = V × C × A × B Cell score = Value Created × Capture Share × Adoption Speed × Buyability

Why multiplication, not summation

Summation would let a strong V compensate for a weak C. That's the mortgage processors trap: V is huge (banks automate claims, save hundreds of millions per year), but C ≈ 0 — five banks own the borrower relationship, and every saved dollar flows upstream to them, not to the processor. Sum-based scoring would falsely greenlight this. Multiplication punishes any weak component: 9 × 9 × 9 × 0.5 = 364 (Pass), not 27 (which a sum would give). That's the right signal.

The four multipliers

VValue Created — what AI dislodged

What
$/year that AI displaces from incumbent in this cell nationally. Normalized to a 0–10 scale.
Formula
V = normalize(LR + ΔM)
LR
Labor Removed = labor cost × workflow share × avg task replaceability. For each task in the workflow, take its AI exposure score from Karpathy.ai/jobs or Anthropic Economic Index, multiply by task share within workflow, by workflow share in total sub-sector labor. This is Karpathy-grade because κ values are measured, not guessed.
ΔM
Margin/Revenue uplift = revenue × (margin uplift + speed uplift × contribution margin). Pulled from our P&L Before/After AI sheet for 16 verticals; new cells require building it fresh.
Sources
BLS QCEW · Celonis/UiPath workflow catalogs · Karpathy.ai/jobs + Anthropic Economic Index · Bobroff 16-vertical P&L sheet
Why V exists: if AI didn't dislodge value (LR + ΔM ≤ 0), there's nothing to extract. BPO × Customer Support has V=10 (AI removes 90% of agents) — but C then zeroes the whole score, because that value flows to direct-to-enterprise AI vendors, not to BPO operators.

CCapture Share — what we pocket

What
% of V we keep (not the upstream gatekeeper, generic-AI vendor, or regulator). 0–10 scale = 0.0–1.0 × 10.
Formula
C = 10 × π_dist × π_moat
π_dist
Distribution Control ∈ [0, 1]. 0 = upstream gatekeeper owns the customer (banks in mortgage; OEMs in parts; ANGI/Thumbtack aggregators in handyman). 1 = the operator we acquire owns the customer directly (broker in insurance, agency in HomeCare, property manager in PM).
π_moat
Moat strength ∈ [0, 1]. Composite of license/regulation + accumulated proprietary data + physical presence + multi-sided network. 0 = generic-AI vendor does it for 12 months without us. 1 = regulated license + 20yr client history + physical service.
Sources
Operator interviews (revenue channel breakdown) · NAIC state licensing · Vendor failure-mode case studies · Bessemer/Sequoia moat research
Why C is multiplicative inside itself: distribution and moat are both necessary. Strong distribution + no moat → generic AI bypasses us within a year. Strong moat + weak distribution → value flows to whoever owns the customer. Multiplication closes both holes.

AAdoption Speed — how fast the market accepts

What
Speed of AI adoption in the workflow, in years until 50% market adoption. Faster = more $/year extracted before commoditization equalizes margins.
Formula
A = 10 / t_50
Calibration
t_50 = 1 yr → A = 10 (doc parsing in claims, already happening). t_50 = 2 yr → A = 5 (most back-office). t_50 = 5 yr → A = 2 (RN scheduling in HomeCare: cultural + regulatory drag). t_50 = 10 yr → A = 1 (surgery, litigation).
Sources
Anthropic Economic Index (current adoption % as leading indicator) · Historical analogues (cloud 2008→2018; ERP 1995→2005; mobile 2007→2014) · McKinsey AI Adoption Survey · Operator integration interviews
Why A: this is Nicolas's "$/time". Total fund $ = ($/year) × (years in the window). If adoption is slow, the window opens too late for fund horizon. Even a high-V cell with slow A can't deliver returns within an IC's tolerance.

BBuyability — can we enter

What
Whether Bobroff can actually acquire operators: enough targets, willing owners, sane multiples, no PE platforms blocking. Replaces the old "fragmentation as gate".
Formula
B = 10 × (TD × OW × PD) / (1 + CFD)
TD
Target Density: log(N operators in our $1–10M EBITDA band). Not "thousands", but "how many in our band".
OW
Owner Willingness: silver tsunami × succession gap × economic distress. 60+ owner without succession + distressed = high.
PD
Price Discipline: (Public comp − Asking) / Public comp. Positive = we buy below comp.
CFD
Competition For Deals: log(1 + active PE platforms). How many funds already buying these deals.
The Agrosignal test: Russian agri-software had one company on the market. TD low. But OW=1 (owner wanted to sell), PD=1 (entered cheap), CFD=0 (nobody else hunting). Product → B high. We bought. Old S16 "fragmentation ≥ thousands" would have blocked this deal. New formula passes correctly.

Worked example

Insurance Brokerage P&C tier-2 × Document parsing

V = 9LR ≈ $3.6B/yr nationally (claims doc parsing κ=0.95, fraud scoring κ=0.80). ΔM ≈ $1.1B/yr. Total ≈ $4.7B/yr at national scale.
C = 6.4π_dist = 0.85 (broker owns client; carrier doesn't). π_moat = 0.75 (50-state license + accumulated book of business). C = 10 × 0.85 × 0.75.
A = 8t_50 ≈ 1.25 yrs: high adoption pressure (labor savings obvious), moderate friction (compliance), current adoption ~20% (Anthropic Economic Index, Mar 2026).
B = 8.5tier-2 segment: 145K agencies, low PE saturation (PE prefers >$5M EBITDA), strong silver tsunami (3rd-gen owners), median asking 5.5×, public comps 11×.
S = 9 × 6.4 × 8 × 8.5 = 3,917 — Hell-Yes 💚
Vs Insurance tier-1: Same V=9, same A=8, but C drops to ~3.5 (PE-saturated), B drops to 3 (CFD high). S = 9 × 3.5 × 8 × 3 = 756. Five times lower score — same NAICS, opposite verdict. The formula does sub-segment definability mathematically, without a separate hard-gate.

What's NOT in the formula — and why

Fragmentation as hard-gate
Absorbed into B via TD. The Agrosignal one-target case passes the formula. "Fragmentation ≥ thousands" was a 2010s private equity artifact, broken on AI-rollup actionable deals.
CAGR / TAM standalone
CAGR is a metric of static economies. AI changes distribution and cost structure, not market size. TAM is implicit in LR and ΔM — but not as a standalone variable.
"Profitable at SME level" gate
Absorbed into V. If LR + ΔM > 0, the cell creates extractable value by definition. No separate gate needed.
"Why this time is different"
AI as external force (V_AI, κ) answers this directly. If V > 0, AI is rewriting the cell's rules — that is "why now". Observation, not criterion.
Founder access
Not in this formula. It's about the deal (Section B), not the vertical class. A vertical can be Hell-Yes on the map; founder access is then checked when shortlisting the top-10 cells for action.
Geography (US vs EU)
Not needed at vertical-selection. The workflow is the same. PropMgmt UK vs US is a deal-level choice. One map, deal execution localizes.
Anti-pattern as separate gate
Catchment-bound, asset-heavy, COGS-dominant — these all mathematically fail on V (low automatable labor share) or B (high capex blocks acquisition). Vet × any workflow yields S < 200 with no separate "anti-pattern" gate.

Honest weaknesses

1. Normalize(V) is subjective. Anchoring LR + ΔM in $/year to a 0–10 scale requires anchor points that may shift as we add cells. Calibrating on the 16-vertical sheet, but the first 50 new cells will stretch the scale.

2. t_50 is an estimate. Anthropic Economic Index gives current level, but S-curve velocity is estimated from analogues. ±50% error band.

3. π_moat is a composite. License + data + physical + network — four different things compressed into one number. Future version may split it.

4. The map is static, the economy isn't. Recompute quarterly. Doc parsing κ can rise from 0.85 to 0.97 in 6 months, moving Accounting × Doc parsing from V=9 to V=10.

Working cycle

  1. Quarterly — refresh the grid: L_S (BLS), κ (new Anthropic Economic Index), PitchBook PE-saturation snapshot.
  2. Per cell — collect 5 atomic inputs (LR, ΔM, π_dist, π_moat, t_50, π_buy). 30–60 min per cell with operator interview + benchmark.
  3. Compute S — formula applies.
  4. Rank — top-50 cells by S → shortlist for detailed workflow drill.
  5. Top-10 only — full workflow audit: P&L bridge, named operators, founder access verified.
  6. Hell-Yes eligibility — only cells where S ≥ 1000 and every block ≥ 3 (no block "carried" by others).
